A bit about me

I'm a bioinformatics student with a minor in computer science. I'm drawn to problems where biological data meets computation — sequence alignment, gene expression, medical imaging — and I like building the tools that make that data easier to work with.

Most of my recent work sits between data science and computational biology: training models on genomic and imaging datasets, and writing the pipelines that get messy lab data into a shape a model can actually learn from.
